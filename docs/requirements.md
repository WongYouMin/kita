# Functional Requirements

## Dashboard
- The system shall display the total number of customers.
- The system shall display the total number of reward redemptions.
- The system shall display the total number of loyalty transactions.

## Customer Management
- The business shall be able to create a customer.
- The business shall be able to view customer details.
- The business shall be able to update customer details.
- The business shall be able to delete a customer.

## Reward Management
- The business shall be able to create a reward.
- The business shall be able to view reward details.
- The business shall be able to update reward details.
- The business shall be able to delete a reward.

## Loyalty Points
- The business shall be able to award loyalty points to a customer.
- The system shall display a customer's current loyalty points.

## Reward Redemption
- The business shall be able to redeem a reward for a customer.
- The system shall automatically deduct the required loyalty points after a successful redemption.

## Transaction History
- The system shall record every loyalty point award as a transaction.
- The system shall record every reward redemption as a transaction.
- The business shall be able to view the transaction history.
