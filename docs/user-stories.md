# User Stories

## Dashboard
- As a business owner, I want to view the total number of customers to **monitor the growth of my customer base**.
- As a business owner, I want to view the total number of reward redemptions to **evaluate customer engagement** with the loyalty program.
- As a business owner, I want to view the total number of loyalty transactions to **monitor customer participation** in the loyalty program.

## Customer Management
- As a business owner, I want to create a customer to **keep track of their loyalty points**.
- As a business owner, I want to view customer details to **manage and contact customers**.
- As a business owner, I want to update customer details to **ensure the accuracy of customer details**.
- As a business owner, I want to delete a customer to **remove inactive or incorrect records**.

## Reward Management
- As a business owner, I want to create a reward **to provide an incentive** for customers to participate in the loyalty program.
- As a business owner, I want to view reward details to **manage available rewards**.
- As a business owner, I want to update reward details to **ensure the accuracy of reward details**.
- As a business owner, I want to delete a reward to **remove inactive or obsolete rewards**.

## Loyalty Points
- As a business owner, I want to award loyalty points to a customer to **reward their purchases or participation**.
- As a business owner, I want to view a customer's current loyalty points to **verify their points balance** for reward redemption.

## Reward Redemption
- As a business owner, I want to redeem a reward for a customer so that customers can **use their accumulated loyalty points**.

## Transaction History
- As a business owner, I want to view the transaction history to **keep track of loyalty point awards and reward redemptions**.
