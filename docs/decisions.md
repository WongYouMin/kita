# Architecture and Product Decisions

## MVP 1 Focus: Business Dashboard First

### Decision
Phase 1 will focus on basic business management features.

### Reason
Business create loyalty programs, rewards and customer data. It builds the foundation for future customer-facing features.

### Alternative Considered
Build customer mobile app first.

### Rejection Reason
Wihout business and rewards, there is limited value for customers.

---

## No Authentication in Month 1

### Decision
Authentication will not be included in the first development sprint.

### Reason
The primary goal of Phase 1 is understanding React, frontend-backend communication, and core loyalty features.

### Alternative Considered
Implement JWT authentication immediately.

### Rejection Reason
Authentication adds complexity and may slow down learning of core application concepts.
