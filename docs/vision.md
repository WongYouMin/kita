# Kita - Product Vision

## Product Name
Kita

## Product Description
Kita is a customer loyalty management platform built to help businesses create and manage rewarding customer experiences. This project documents my one-year journey of learning frontend development, product thinking, and software engineering through building a real-world application.

## Problem Statement
Many small and medium businesses recognize the value of customer loyalty programs, but implementing and managing them can be challenging. Existing solutions are often industry-specific, expensive, or overly complex. As a result, businesses either do not implement a loyalty program or fail to provide a rewarding experience that encourages repeat customers.

## Target Users
**Primary Users**
- Business Owners
- Managers
- Staff

**Secondary Users**
- Customers

## Product Vision
To connect businesses and customers through a unified, customizable loyalty platform.

## Core Value Proposition
Kita is a unified loyalty platform that helps businesses build customizable loyalty programs while giving customers one place to manage all their memberships and rewards.

## Initial Scope (MVP)
**Month 1**
Admin should be able to:
1. Register customers
2. Create rewards
3. Award loyalty points
4. Redeem rewards
5. View transaction history
