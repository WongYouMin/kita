# Existing Products Analysis

## Product
BonusLink 

### Strengths

#### Security
- Requires verified phone number and email.
- Helps prevent fake accounts and abuse.

#### Responsive Design
- Works well across mobile devices.
- Provides a consistent user experience regardless of screen size.

#### AI Chatbot
- Assists users with common questions and troubleshooting.
- Reduces the need for customer support.

#### User Experience
- Clean and minimalist interface.
- Consistent design across the application.
- Easy for first-time users to navigate.

#### Navigation
- Only five main menu items.
- Simple and descriptive menu names.
- Reduces cognitive load for users.

#### Flexible Rewards
- Rewards can be redeemed using points or purchased directly.
- Gives customers more redemption options.

#### Merchant Collaboration
- Multiple businesses participate in the same loyalty ecosystem.
- Customers can earn points across participating merchants.

---

### Areas to Improve

- Reward redemption process could be simplified.
- Some features require several steps to complete.
- Business management features are less prominent than customer-facing features.
- Limited customization for each merchant's loyalty program.
- Can read [The Loyalty App That Lost Its Spark](https://akmalhakimahmad.medium.com/the-loyalty-app-that-lost-its-spark-2e16f56a1df0)

---

### What Kita Can Learn

#### Product Design
- Keep navigation simple.
- Focus on an intuitive user experience.
- Prioritize consistency across all pages.

#### Technical
- Build responsive layouts from the beginning.
- Design the backend with future scalability in mind.
- Plan authentication and security properly.

#### Business
- Support multiple business types in the future.
- Allow merchants to configure loyalty rules instead of using fixed ones.
- Make reward redemption straightforward and transparent.

## Product
Square Loyalty

### Strengths

#### Business Integration
- Integrates directly with Square's POS ecosystem.
- Reduces the need for separate loyalty software.

#### Simple Loyalty Setup
- Businesses create loyalty programs quickly with minimal configuration.
- Customizable rewards earning rules.
- Suitable for small and medium enterprises without needing technical expertise.

#### Flexible Ways to Earn Points
- Earn points based on visit frequency, amount spent, specific items or categories.
- Supports VIP tiers, digital passes and promotions to encourage repeat purchases.

#### Convenient Customer Enrollment
- Customers can enroll through Apple Wallet, QR codes, online registration or invoices.
- Reduces friction during registration.

#### Multiple Redemption Options
- Customers can redeem rewards both online and in physical stores.
- Offers an omnichannel loyalty experience.

---

### Areas to Improve

#### Square Ecosystem Exclusivity
- Best suited for businesses using Square POS.
- Businesses with other POS systems may face integration limitations.

#### No Support for Multi-Merchant Loyalty
- Loyalty programs are generally designed for individual businesses.
- Customers cannot easily earn points and redeem rewards across multiple independent merchants.

#### Limited Merchant Insights
- The platform primarily focuses on operational metrics in reporting.
- More actionable customer insights, such as churn prediction or personalized recommendations, could help to improve customer retention.

#### Limited Customer Discovery
- Customers typically enroll in the loyalty program after interacting with a business.
- The platform offers limited features for discovering nearby participating merchants or exploring available rewards.

---

### What Kita Can Learn

#### Centralized Loyalty Across Businesses
- Allow customers to use a single loyalty account across multiple participating SMEs.
- Allow each merchant to manage their own loyalty program independently.

#### Merchant-Centric Dashboard
- Provide merchants with actionable insights including: repeat customer rate, top loyal customers and most redeemed rewards.

#### Faster Program Management
- Enable merchants to create, modify and launch loyalty program campaigns without technical knowledge.

#### Better Customer Experience
- Offer a unified mobile application where customers can discover nearby participating merchants, track points from multiple businesses, browse available rewards and redeem rewards digitally

#### AI-Powered Recommendations
- Provide intelligent recommendations, such as suggesting promotions based on customer behavior and identifying the best-performing reward types.
