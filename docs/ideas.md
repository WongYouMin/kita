# Product Ideas

## Multi-Business Platform
Allow multiple businesses to create and manage their own loyalty programs.

## Customer Application
Customers can:
- Browse participating businesses,
- Join loyalty programs.
- Track loyalty points.
- Redeem rewards.

## Business Discovery
Customers gets recommended of nearby businesses based on location and interest.

## Reward Fulfillment
Businesses choose how rewards are fulfilled:
- In-store pickup
- Merchant delivery
- Digital rewards

## Loyalty Rules
Businesses can customize:
- Points earning rules
- Redemption rules
- Birthday rewards
- Referral rewards

## Analytics
- Customer segmentation
- Churn risk prediction
- AI loyalty insights
